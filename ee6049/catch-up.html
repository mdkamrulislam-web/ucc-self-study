Every block, the same four steps

Each day below is one block. Work through it in this order.

  1. 1

    Watch Razavi

    At 1.25–1.5× speed. When he gives a 90-second quiz, pause and try it.

  2. 2

    Read the slides

    They are the exam version. They add the body effect, gds and the Gm/Gout shortcut.

  3. 3

    Try the problem

    Do the end-of-section problem on paper before you open the solution.

  4. 4

    Check

    Compare with the guide lesson or the solution. Stuck for 20 minutes? Rewatch the Razavi part first.
