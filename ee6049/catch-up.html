Every block, the same four steps

Each day below is one block. Work through it in this order.

  1. 1

    Watch Razavi

    At 1.5× speed, for the intuition. When he gives a 90-second quiz, pause and try it.

  2. 2

    Watch the lecture

    Your own recording at 1.25×, slides open. It's the exam version: gds, the body effect, the Gm/Gout shortcut.

  3. 3

    Try the problem

    On paper, before the part of a recording that solves it. The plan always lists the try first.

  4. 4

    Check

    Against the lecturer's worked solution, the solution PDF, then the guide lesson. Stuck for 20 minutes? Rewatch the Razavi part.
